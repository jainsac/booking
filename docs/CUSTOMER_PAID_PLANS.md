# Customer Paid Plans

Customer plans are designed around convenience, premium tooling, and access to member-only business benefits.

## Free
- Standard booking
- Live queue number
- ETA and people ahead
- Push notifications
- Basic booking history
- Up to 1 active booking per business
- No member-only business discounts

## Plus
Suggested price: ₹49/month or ₹399/year
- Everything in Free
- Up to 3 active bookings across businesses
- Family/dependent profiles
- Book for another person
- Favorite businesses
- Advanced ETA alerts
- Calendar reminders
- Easy reschedule/rebook where the business permits it
- Ad-free experience
- Extended booking history
- Access to participating-business member discounts
- Discounted Home/On-site Visit add-ons where the business enables them

## Premium
Suggested price: ₹99/month or ₹799/year
- Everything in Plus
- Up to 5 active bookings
- Priority access to newly released app-booking slots, only when the business enables this feature
- Smart arrival reminder based on live ETA
- Automatic late-arrival protection/rebooking workflow where the business supports it
- Recurring/frequent booking shortcuts
- Multi-person/group booking tools where supported
- Premium queue analytics/history
- Faster in-app support
- Premium/member discount benefits from participating businesses
- Better/discounted Home/On-site Visit add-on pricing where the business enables it

## Business discounts
Business discounts are a paid-member benefit. Free customers do not receive member-only discounts.
Each business controls its own offer:
- discount percentage or fixed amount
- eligible service(s)
- Plus and/or Premium eligibility
- eligible days/times
- minimum order/booking value
- usage limit per customer
- validity/expiry
- whether the discount can combine with other offers

The customer must see the discount and its conditions before payment.

## Home / On-site Visit — separate paid add-on
For businesses that can send a professional to the customer's location, Home/On-site Visit is a separate purchasable service/add-on, not part of the normal queue booking fee.

Examples:
- Doctor/health professional home visit
- Salon/beauty professional home service
- Technician/repair professional visit
- Fitness/trainer home session
- Consultant/professional customer-location visit
- Other businesses where an on-site service is operationally possible

Business controls:
- whether home/on-site visits are enabled
- which services support visits
- visit fee
- service price
- travel/distance fee, if applicable
- serviceable areas/radius or postal codes
- available days and time windows
- advance-booking requirement
- cancellation/rescheduling rules
- number of visits/staff available
- whether Free users can purchase the visit at full price
- Plus/Premium member discount percentage or fixed member price
- blackout dates/areas

Recommended customer pricing logic:
- Free: standard business-set Home/On-site Visit price.
- Plus: discounted member price when the business enables a Plus discount.
- Premium: discounted member price, potentially better than Plus when the business chooses.
- The business may choose not to offer a member discount.

Important: The membership discount applies to the Home/On-site Visit charge only when the business has explicitly configured it. It does not automatically discount unrelated services.

## Fairness and queue rules
Paid customers should not silently jump ahead of offline or other online customers. Any queue-priority capability must be explicitly enabled by the business and shown before checkout/booking.

Home/On-site Visit should normally bypass the physical walk-in queue because the professional travels to the customer's location. The business controls visit capacity and availability separately from the in-store queue.

## Strongest monetization features
1. Family/dependent profiles
2. Multiple active bookings
3. Early access to business-released slots
4. Smart ETA and arrival alerts
5. Member-only business discounts
6. Discounted Home/On-site Visits
7. Ad-free experience
8. Rebook/reschedule automation
9. Recurring booking shortcuts
10. Booking history and receipts

## Recommended launch
Start with Free + Plus. Add Premium after real usage data shows which convenience features customers repeatedly use. Avoid charging for the basic live queue itself.
